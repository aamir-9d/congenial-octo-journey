# 2026-11-26, second session: section spacing

## Changed (branch `section-spacing`)
- `--section-pad` from `clamp(72px, 8vw, 124px)` to `clamp(48px, 5.5vw, 88px)`.
  It sits above and below every section, so the empty band between two
  sections falls from 144px to 96px on a phone and from about 230px to about
  160px at 1440px wide, across the whole site.
- Service pages: the explainer cards have no heading of their own, so their
  section drops its top padding and follows the mistakes cards with one
  section-pad (48px on a phone) instead of two.
- A test fails if the token grows past 56px (phone) or 96px (desktop).

  home, /services, a service page, /blog. Screenshots checked at both widths.
