The findings

Nine faults, and the fix for each

Each card says what the fault does to the numbers and links to the service that fixes it. Bars show how often it appeared.

01In every audit

ATT handled badly

The prompt appeared at first launch, or the MMP SDK started before the user answered, or the consent rate was implausible (near zero or 100%). Installs arrive without an advertising ID and iOS campaigns look weaker than they are.

Fix: ATT & consent timing →
02In nearly every audit

Revenue or purchase events missing or wrong

Revenue not tracked, sent without the right value or currency, or the purchase event never reached the ad platforms. Campaigns optimise without the signal that matters most.

Fix: Event tracking plan →
03In more than half

SKAN conversion values or reporting not set up

Conversion values missing or unmapped, window 1 capacity unused, or the SKAN reporting connection off. iOS campaigns get little or no usable postback data.

Fix: SKAN & AdAttributionKit setup →
04In more than half

Re-engagement attribution misconfigured

Re-engagement windows and inactivity rules not set for how the app is used, so retargeting takes credit for users who were already active, or gets none.

Fix: Mobile attribution audit →
05In more than half

Ad partner postbacks and integrations incomplete

In-app event postbacks off, partner connections set up the old way, or conversion goals missing in the ad platform. The networks cannot optimise towards the events you care about.

Fix: Event tracking plan →
06In half

Uninstall measurement off

No view of which channels bring users who leave within days.

Fix: Mobile attribution audit →
07In some audits

View-through attribution off or misconfigured

Installs driven by video and display ads are credited elsewhere or to organic.

Fix: Mobile attribution audit →
08In some audits

Web-to-app tracking missing

Installs that start on the website lose their campaign and look organic.

Fix: Mobile attribution audit →
09In some audits

Renewals not sent from the server

Subscription revenue after the first charge never reaches the MMP, so long-term ROAS looks too low.

Fix: Subscription revenue tracking →

Counting rule
An audit counts once for a finding, however many checks in that area needed fixing.
Method

How these were counted

  • Each audit is a workbook of checks marked "Looks good", "Needs review" or "Needs fixes".
  • An audit counts once for a finding if at least one check in that area needed fixing. "Needs review" is not counted.
  • Only the attribution platform checks are used. Analytics checks are left out.
  • A check copied word for word between audit workbooks is not counted, and nor is one marked not applicable after discussion.
  • Every match was read by hand before it was counted.
Limits

What this cannot show

These are apps whose teams asked for an audit, so they are more likely than average to have problems. It is a small sample: read the counts as what to check first, not as an industry rate.
