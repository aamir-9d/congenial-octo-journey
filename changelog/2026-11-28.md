  out, as if colours were missing. An audit of the live pages found six
  faults: page, card and band within 1.07:1 of each other; no dark section;
  the second hue lost and amber text turned brown; hairlines at 1.19:1; the
  corner glow reading as a stain on white; icon tiles with no body.

  white cards lift, stronger lines, a warmer amber for text, and new tokens

  the lead card of a group and the closing ask take the amber wash; panels
  behind charts take the blue wash.
- New `.island` class: a dark section on a light page. Used for the
  calculator preview on the homepage and the footer on every page.
- The dark theme is unchanged.
- `scripts/check-contrast.mjs` checks text on the two washes as well.
