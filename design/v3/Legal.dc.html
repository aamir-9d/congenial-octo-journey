Privacy

This page describes exactly what this website collects, which third parties see it, and how long it is kept.

We run a measurement consultancy, so it would be a poor look to be vague about our own. No generic template, no "we may collect certain information".

The short version

Every visit is counted by Cloudflare Web Analytics, which uses no cookies and keeps no personal data. Until you press Got it, nothing else about your visit is sent to an analytics or advertising service. If you press it, Google Analytics and Google Ads can see that you visited. If you submit the form or book a call, we get what you typed plus the ad click you arrived on.

Who we are

E2E Apps. Contact hello@e2eapp.com for anything on this page, including a request to delete your data. A person reads that address.

What we collect, and when

If you just read the page without pressing Got it

Cloudflare Web Analytics counts the page view: the page, the site that sent you, your browser type and country. It sets no cookie, does not identify you, and does not follow you across sites. Nothing else is sent.

If you press Got it

Google Analytics 4 records the pages you view, how far you scroll, which sections you reach, and how you use the payback calculator — which model you pick, the values you set, and the breakeven it returns. It is not linked to your name unless you also contact us.

We do not collect precise location, we do not fingerprint devices, we do not buy or enrich data about you, and we do not sell anything to anyone.

If you arrived from an advert

Advertising platforms append a click identifier to the link. We read these from the address bar and keep them in a first-party cookie for 90 days:

gclidgbraidwbraidfbclidli_fat_idttclid

If you later submit the form, that identifier is attached, which is how we tell which advert produced an enquiry. This is the same mechanic we build for clients; it seemed dishonest to sell it without running it.

If you submit the form or book a call

We receive your name, work email, app store URL if you gave one, the ad-spend band you picked, your message, the stored click identifiers, the page you submitted from, and the country your request came from. Booking a call sends your name, email and chosen slot to Calendly.
