02 · Colour

One accent, four greys, no second hue

The palette is a near-black ground, four text greys and a single amber. There is no secondary brand colour and no gradient fills — depth comes from surface steps and from blurred radial glows behind a section, never from a gradient across a card.

Ground and surface

Four steps, all near-neutral. A card is one step above its ground; a tile inside a card is one step above that. Never more than three steps in one view.

03 · Typography

Why your numbers don't reconcile

Three places the money goes missing.

06 · Data visualisation

One saturated line in the frame

True revenue is the only saturated thing on a chart. What the dashboard sees is grey and dashed — literally the less real of the two. There is no red, and no warm/cool split.

Axis labels are HTML, never SVG <text>

The chart SVG scales to its column, so text inside it shrinks with the viewBox and becomes illegible. Labels are positioned over the SVG as percentages of its box, at real px sizes. The y-axis gutter is a fixed flex column outside the viewBox — 54px on desktop, 46px on mobile.

07 · Motion

Opacity and transform only

Nothing in the system animates a property that triggers layout, so there is no CLS cost. One easing curve throughout. Everything stops after its entrance except the two hero glows and the loop's travelling signal.

Hero entrance Pill, headline, lead, CTAs and chips rise into place in sequence. fade + 16px rise · 600ms delays 0/80/160/240/320ms
Scroll reveal Section headers and cards on a scroll-driven timeline, no JS and no class gate. animation-timeline: view() entry 4% → entry 55% · 650ms
Hero backdrop Two blurred radial glows drifting, plus one accent line sweeping the top edge. 28s / 36s ease-in-out sweep 11s linear
The loop signal Desktop: a dot travels the circle. Mobile: each rail node lights in sequence. animateMotion 11s nodes 5.2s · delays 0/1.3/2.6/3.9s
Chart draw-on The true-revenue path draws from left to right on first paint. pathLength=1 · dashoffset 1→0 1.2–1.3s · delay 300–400ms
Interactive states Button fill and glow, card lift, input border, chip tint. 160–200ms · background, box-shadow, transform only

Easing cubic-bezier(0.22, 0.61, 0.36, 1) — the only curve in the system.
Every keyframe sits behind prefers-reduced-motion.

09 · Rules

Do and don't

Do

Fill the primary button. Amber on transparent was tried and rejected — the filled button is the brand’s one confident gesture.

Set every figure in tabular numerals, in both families.

Centre section headers and cap them in ch units. Mixed left- and centre-aligned headers is what made the old page feel unaligned.

Draw card outlines as a 1px box-shadow, not a border.

Put chart axis labels in HTML over the SVG, never in SVG text.

Give every tap target 44px on mobile, and every native control margin: 0.

Don't

Do not add a second brand hue. If something needs to stand apart from amber, use a grey step or a size change.

Do not use red — not for the dashboard line, not for errors in a chart. Grey and dashed carries “less real” better.

Do not gradient-fill a card or a button. Gradients exist only as blurred radial glows behind a section and as the section wash.

Do not set amber text on a light ground — it falls to 2.2:1.

Do not stack shadows. On this ground, elevation is one hairline plus one ambient shadow.

Do not put white-space: nowrap on a row or a label — only on the value it protects.
