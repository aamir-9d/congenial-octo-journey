Six more, read against the category.

Semrush, Ahrefs, AppTweak, Sensor Tower, RevenueCat and Adapty share a grammar: one compact geometric glyph beside a bold sans wordmark, single-colour capable, with an implied direction of travel — up, or forward. None of them tries to be clever. These six sit in that grammar but say the thing none of them say, which is end to end: a start, a path, and a finish.

Original geometry throughout — no competitor's mark is reproduced or approximated. Round 1 is unchanged below.
